Aevum

Local-First Intelligence

Aevum is a local-first intelligence system for private thinking, capture, and reflection. Your thoughts, captures, and memory graph are designed to be processed on-device rather than sent to the internet for reasoning.

To enable local execution, model artifacts may be securely downloaded to your device. Personal thinking and memory-graph reasoning are intended to remain local to the app experience.
